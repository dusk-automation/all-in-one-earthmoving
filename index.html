About us

One local crew with the machines, the operators and the trucks.

All-In-One Earthmoving has been moving earth across the Northern Rivers since 2007, from a base near Alstonville.

Between the excavators, the tip truck and the float, one call can cover the dig, the walls, the cart-away and getting the machine to site. That means fewer contractors to line up, and one team who knows the job from start to finish.

Clients mention the same things again and again: a precise operator, clear communication every step of the way, and a site left clean and tidy.

Services

What we do

From clearing the block to the last load out, for homes, builders and rural properties.

  1. 01

    Excavation & site works

    House cuts, footings and general site works, levelled and ready for the next trade.

  2. 02

    Rock & block retaining walls

    Sandstone and block walls that hold a slope and turn it into usable, level ground.

  3. 03

    Pool digs

    Digging out for new pools and lifting fibreglass shells into place, even over a fence line.

  4. 04

    Clearing & removals

    Clearing sites and breaking out what has to go, like an old in-ground pool.

  5. 05

    Tip truck hire

    Carting spoil away and bringing materials in, with the truck and driver together.

  6. 06

    Float moves

    Moving machinery from one site to the next on the float.
